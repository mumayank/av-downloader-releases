Copy a link, get a bubble

Tap "Copy link" in your favourite video or community app and a small gradient bubble floats up for ten seconds. Ignore it, or drag it away, and nothing happens.

One-tap download

Tap the bubble and the video or images download in the background, with the title, description and top comment gathered into a tidy caption. Text-only posts become clean, readable images.

Progress rings

Each download shows a small floating ring that fills as it goes. Run several at once, move them anywhere, or drag one to the X to cancel it.

A share card, one at a time

When a download is ready, a compact dark card slides up with a preview and big buttons for your favourite chat apps, or any app on your phone. The rest of your screen keeps working.

No ads, no sponsor segments

Ads are filtered out of everything the app reads, and sponsor and self-promo segments are cut out of videos before you share them.

Updates itself

The version sits under the app name with a refresh button. When a new build is out, it downloads quietly and turns into an Update button.

Your phone, your business.

There's no server behind this app. Nothing to sign up for, nothing collected, nothing sold.
